# Optimal eight-point configurations for all exponents $s>-2$

These independent certificates prove that the optimized square antiprism uniquely
minimizes the energy of eight points on the unit sphere, up to orthogonal
transformations and relabeling. Both packages cover the logarithmic case $s=0$.
The energy and the Lean theorem are stated in the [repository README](../README.md#lean-verified-statements).

## Negative exponents

For $-2\lt s\le0$, five interval certificates cover $q=(s+2)/2$ from 0 to 1.
See [Negative_s](Negative_s) and its [replay instructions](Negative_s/README.md#recompute-the-complete-extension).

## Nonnegative exponents

For $s\ge0$, 111 finite cells cover $[0,124]$ and an analytic argument with
exact checks covers $s\ge124$. See [Nonnegative_s](Nonnegative_s) and its
[replay instructions](Nonnegative_s/CAP.md).
